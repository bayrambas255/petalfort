Your garden stays yours

Petalfort works without an account and plays fully offline. It has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics. The tutorial and the first two regions are free; the Full Garden is a one-time unlock, and optional crystal packs are sold through the App Store. Nothing sold is random, and every mission can be won without Jokers or permanent upgrades. Progress is stored on your iPhone and, when iCloud is on, backed up to your own iCloud rather than a Petalfort server.
