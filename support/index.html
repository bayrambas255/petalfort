Faster troubleshooting

What makes a useful report?

Share only technical details that help reproduce the issue.

  • Petalfort version, shown in Settings;
  • iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the mission or screen where the problem occurred;
  • short, numbered steps that reproduce the behavior; and
  • what you expected and what happened instead.

Common questions

Quick answers

Does Petalfort need an account or internet connection?

No. Petalfort works without an account and plays fully offline. Purchases and the optional iCloud backup use your Apple Account through the App Store and iCloud.

Does the game contain ads, tracking, or in-app purchases?

Petalfort has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics. The tutorial and the first two regions are free. The Full Garden (the last two regions) is a one-time purchase, and crystal packs are optional. Nothing sold is random and every mission can be won without buying anything.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings › Purchases & Backup › Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. The Full Garden and the Starter Pack are one-time purchases and are also restored automatically on a new install.

Can crystals I bought be restored?

Crystal packs are consumable, so the App Store cannot restore them the way it restores one-time purchases. Your crystals are kept in your garden: when iCloud is on, they are included in your iCloud backup and come back when you restore it. If iCloud is off and the app is deleted, unspent crystals cannot be recovered.

I played before purchases were added. Do I have to pay for the Full Garden?

No. If you first downloaded Petalfort from the App Store before in-app purchases were introduced, or your saved garden had already reached a Full Garden mission, the full campaign stays open for you at no cost.

A purchase is pending or did not arrive. What should I do?

If Ask to Buy is on, the item is delivered after it is approved; open Petalfort to receive it. If a purchase was interrupted, reopen Petalfort; the App Store delivers unfinished purchases automatically. If an item is still missing, use Restore Purchases, then email support. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Is my progress backed up or synchronized?

When iCloud is on, Petalfort backs up your garden — progress, crystals, purchases, Jokers, achievements, and statistics — to your own iCloud. You can also back up right away from Settings › Purchases & Backup. After a reinstall or on a new iPhone, the game offers to restore it. Petalfort never overwrites a different iCloud garden without asking. There is no developer server or account.

What does Reset Campaign Progress remove?

It clears campaign progress and the current game checkpoint. It does not erase preferences, earned inventory or currency, achievements, or statistics.

What are Playtest Insights?

They are an optional local archive of authored gameplay events and numeric outcomes. The setting is off by default. Petalfort retains at most 1,500 events from the latest 30 days and never uploads the archive automatically. A report leaves the device only when you explicitly use the iOS share sheet and select a destination.

How do I change the game’s language?

Petalfort is available in English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish (Latin America), Portuguese (Brazil), Traditional Chinese, Turkish, French (Canada), and Arabic. Open Settings › Language in the game, or the iOS Settings app › Apps › Petalfort › Language, and choose a language. The privacy policy and this website are in English.

How can I report an accessibility problem?

Use the support form above and choose the accessibility category. Include the iPhone model, iOS version, text-size or accessibility setting involved, the affected screen, and reproduction steps. Do not include personal information.
